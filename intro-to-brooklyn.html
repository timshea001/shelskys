Good to know

Questions, answered.

Will it show up fresh?

Yes. Everything is packed cold the morning it ships, in insulated packaging built to hold temperature. Most of the country is one to two days from our kitchen by ground. Pick your delivery date at checkout so it lands when you want it.

Why not just order bagels?

You can. But a bagel with nothing on it is breakfast. A bagel with Gaspé Nova, scallion cream cheese, and whitefish salad on the side is the reason people line up on Court Street. The Intro is the whole sandwich, shipped free, for less than the bagels and fish would cost you separately once shipping is added.

Can I swap the salmon or the schmears?

The Intro is fixed so we can pack it fast and ship it free. It is the salmon and the schmears we would pick for you anyway. Everything else on the counter, the sable, the pastrami salmon, the gravlax, is one click away for your next order.

How many people does it feed?

Three to four for a proper brunch, or two people across two mornings. Six bagels, a quarter pound of salmon, two quarter-pound schmears, and a quarter pound of whitefish salad.

Can I send it as a gift?

Yes. Ship it straight to them and add a gift message at checkout. It is the box a transplanted New Yorker actually wants.

What if I'm local?

Lucky you. Order for local delivery or pickup at 141 Court Street.
