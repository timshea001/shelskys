Yes. Everything is packed cold the morning it ships, in insulated packaging built to hold temperature. Most of the country is one to two days from our kitchen by ground — pick your delivery date at checkout so it lands when you want it.

Six to eight comfortably. A dozen bagels, four schmears, and a half-pound of whitefish salad is plenty for a brunch table or a houseful of weekend guests.

Absolutely. Add the reusable wooden gift crate at checkout and ship it straight to them — it's the brunch a transplanted New Yorker actually wants.
